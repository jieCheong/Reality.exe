ORBIT routines

Your child says “good morning” or “good night” on the call, and ORBIT runs that routine. Each step has a time limit: the monster shows up near the end, and running out of time costs a life.

Routine

Steps

“Doing” steps: ORBIT watches your child do it (brushing teeth, reading). “Show” steps: your child shows the item to the camera when done (lunchbox, folded blanket). Items and actions: lowercase words, up to 31 letters. Up to 12 steps.
